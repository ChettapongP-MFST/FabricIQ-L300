# FabricIQ-L300 — Microsoft Fabric IQ Hands-on Labs (Level 300)

Intensive hands-on labs and workshops for **Microsoft Fabric IQ**, designed for practitioners who already understand the basics (level 200) and want to go deep into ontology design, cross-domain semantics, and AI agents grounded on Fabric IQ.

> Primary documentation: <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/iq/>


## Industry context

All labs use a **Thai retail-banking** mock dataset (THB, Thai customer names, Bangkok-centric branches) to make scenarios tangible.

## Three business domains

| Domain | Focus | Key entities |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Customer** | Who the customer is | Customer, Address, Segment, KYC, Contact |
| **B — Products** | What they hold / owe | Product, Account, Card, Loan, Deposit, Investment |
| **C — Sales & Engagement** | How we reach & serve them | Branch, Employee, Campaign, Response, Ticket, Interaction, Lead |

Cross-domain links are introduced in Lab 2 (e.g., `Customer (A) ↔ Account (B)`, `Customer (A) ↔ CampaignResponse (C)`).

## Prerequisites

See [workshop/prerequisites.md](workshop/prerequisites.md). Short version:
- Microsoft Fabric capacity (F2 or trial) with Fabric IQ enabled
- A Fabric workspace assigned to that capacity
- Permissions to create Lakehouse, Eventhouse, Ontology, Data Agent
- (Optional Lab 3) Microsoft 365 tenant with Teams + Copilot Studio access
